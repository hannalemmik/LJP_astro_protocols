# Preparation of LB broth and LB-kanamycin agar plates

**Author:** Hanna Lemmik  
**Source:** Lab notebook, pp. 14 (22–23 Sept 2026)

## Abstract

This protocol describes how to prepare a 1000× kanamycin stock solution (50 mg/ml), sterile LB broth, and LB agar plates containing 50 µg/ml kanamycin for selection of transformed *E. coli*.

## Guidelines

- Work under the circulating-air (laminar flow) cabinet for all sterile handling. Sterile bottles, tubes and plates should only be opened inside the cabinet, otherwise sterility is lost.
- Autoclave LB broth on the day it is made. Do not leave unsterilised broth at room temperature overnight before autoclaving.
- Kanamycin is heat- and light-sensitive: add it only after the agar has cooled to about 50 °C, and store stocks protected from light.
- Volume guide: 20 ml of agar per plate, so 400 ml makes 20 plates.

## Materials

- LB broth: tryptone 10 g/L, yeast extract 5 g/L, NaCl 10 g/L (or premixed LB broth powder as per manufacturer)
- Agar (bacteriological), 15 g per litre of LB
- Kanamycin sulfate powder (MW 582.58 g/mol; lot #SLCK0506)
- Ultrapure water (sterile)
- 0.22 µm syringe filters and sterile syringes
- Sterile 1.5 ml microcentrifuge tubes
- Sterile 90 mm Petri dishes
- Autoclavable glass bottles
- Water bath or incubator set to 50 °C (optional, for cooling agar)

## Safety

Use heat-resistant gloves when handling autoclaved media. Kanamycin is a potential sensitiser and reproductive toxin: wear gloves and avoid inhaling the powder.

## Procedure

### A. Kanamycin stock solution (1000×, 50 mg/ml)

1. Weigh 1 g kanamycin sulfate and dissolve in 20 ml ultrapure water to give 50 mg/ml.
2. Under the cabinet, filter-sterilise the solution with a 0.22 µm syringe filter.
3. Aliquot into sterile 1.5 ml tubes.
   *500 µl is the amount needed for 500 ml of LB agar, so 500 µl aliquots work well.*
4. Label and store at −20 °C, protected from light. The stock keeps for up to 1 year.

### B. LB broth

1. Dissolve the LB components (10 g tryptone, 5 g yeast extract, 10 g NaCl per litre) in ultrapure water and make up to the final volume.
2. Autoclave the same day.
   *Do not leave the broth at room temperature overnight before autoclaving.*
3. Once cooled, store sterile LB broth at 4 °C for 2–3 months.

### C. LB agar plates with kanamycin (50 µg/ml)

1. Add 15 g agar per litre of LB broth (e.g. 6 g agar in 400 ml LB for 20 plates; 7.5 g in 500 ml for 25 plates).
   *Use a bottle at least twice the volume of the medium.*
2. Autoclave.
3. Cool the agar to about 50 °C, warm to the touch but not hot.
4. Under the cabinet, add kanamycin stock 1:1000 to a final concentration of 50 µg/ml (500 µl of 50 mg/ml stock per 500 ml agar). Swirl gently to mix without making bubbles.
5. Pour 20 ml per plate.
   *Pour consistently: the first batch (23 Sept 2026) gave 16 plates of variable thickness from 500 ml.*
6. Leave the plates to solidify at room temperature with the lids on.
7. Label the plates (medium, antibiotic, date) and store inverted at 4 °C for up to 3 weeks.
